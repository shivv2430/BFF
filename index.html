The 3 Golden Rules

1. Audience of One

If it solves a problem for one person you care about, it is 100% worth building.

2. Keep It Small

You can build it in an afternoon. A simple list or button is better than a big complex app.

3. Give It Like a Gift

Send the link in a text message. Seeing their smile is the best part of coding.
